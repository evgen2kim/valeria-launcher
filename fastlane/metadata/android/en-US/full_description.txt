Valeria Launcher is a minimal, list-based Android home screen based on Victoria Launcher. Your favorites can appear or disappear as your day changes.

• Set visibility rules for apps, shortcuts, and folders using Wi-Fi networks, time windows, or connected headphones.
• Hide a favorite from the home screen after a set number of daily sessions. You can still find it in the app list and search.
• Open search with a swipe up, or swipe in from the edge and jump to a letter in the alphabetical app list.
• Customize the layout and add widgets, folders, and icon packs.

Rules are checked on your device while the launcher is on screen. Android requires location permission to read Wi-Fi network names and usage access to count sessions. Both are optional and requested when you set up the relevant rule. Valeria has no ads, tracking, or internet permission.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
